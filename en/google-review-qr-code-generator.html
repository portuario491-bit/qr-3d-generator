Who this is for

Any business with a Google listing (restaurants, shops, salons, repair shops, rentals) that wants more reviews without asking every customer out loud. The QR code jumps straight to the star rating — no extra steps, no searching for your business by hand.

Why 3D instead of a paper sign

A two-color stand with your QR code stands up at the register or on the table, looks a lot more polished than a laminated sheet, and won't get lost in the counter clutter. You can add your business name embossed below the code.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get my direct "leave a review" link?

Find your business on Google Maps or Google Search, open your listing, tap the star rating or "Write a review", and copy the short URL Google gives you (it usually starts with g.page or contains "writereview").

Is this compliant with Google's review policies?

Asking for reviews neutrally — without offering anything in exchange for a positive review, and without filtering to only ask happy customers — is allowed under Google's policies. Avoid incentivizing reviews or asking selectively.

Where should I place the stand for best results?

At the counter, next to the register, or on the table at the end of the visit — the moment the customer has already experienced your service and is most willing to rate it.

Do I need my own 3D printer?

Not to generate the file — the tool is free. To print it, yes, you'll need a 3D printer; if you don't have one yet, we compare models here (in Spanish).

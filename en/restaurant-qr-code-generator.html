Who this is for

Restaurants, bars, cafés and food trucks who want to display a menu without reprinting it every time a price or dish changes. Also works for daily specials, allergen menus, or a takeout ordering link.

Why a 3D stand instead of a paper sign

A two-color stand — base and QR code — stands up on the table at an angle designed to print without supports, holds up to daily restaurant use, and won't get wet or curl the way laminated paper does. You can optionally add your business name embossed below the QR code.

Frequently asked questions

Can I update the menu without reprinting the stand?

Yes — if the QR code points to a webpage (digital menu, a PDF on your site, a public Google Drive link), you can update that page's content anytime without touching the printed stand. The QR code keeps pointing to the same link.

How many stands do I need?

One per table is typical, though some places prefer one per two tables or a single one at the entrance. The piece takes minutes to generate and print, so make as many as you need.

Is it food-service friendly / easy to clean?

Standard FDM plastics (PLA or PETG) wipe clean with a damp cloth. For heavy use with strong disinfectants, PETG holds up better than PLA.

Do I need my own 3D printer?

Not to generate the file — the tool is free. To print it, yes, you'll need a 3D printer; if you don't have one yet, we compare models here (in Spanish).
